Layout options 1-3 are the three Devta diagrams supplied: the boundary is rotated to the North angle, stretched onto the square mandala (Brahma at the area centroid, same direction/ratio in every direction) and each point takes the Devta of the diagram cell it lands on. Which textual source (Mayamata / Manasara / Brihat Samhita) each diagram follows is not confirmed - they are labelled by diagram only.
Method (Radial layout): the centre is the area centroid of the boundary polygon. In every direction the distance from the centre to the boundary is split into Brahma (inner), the inner ring (12 Devtas) and the outer band (32 Devtas). The outer 32 are 11.25° wedges numbered clockwise from NE: E1–E8 = 14–21, S1–S8 = 22–29, W1–W8 = 30–37, N1–N8 = 38–45 (E1 starts at 45° from North). The inner ring uses the same compass sectors: each side Devta spans ±26.565° around its cardinal direction, and each corner pair splits at the diagonal. Names follow your reference sheet; edit NAMES in the script to change spelling.
