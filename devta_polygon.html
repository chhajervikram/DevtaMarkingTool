6. Marking

Upload a plan (or draw on the blank canvas), mark the boundary, then press OK.
Method: the centre is the area centroid of the boundary polygon. In every direction the distance from the centre to the boundary is split into Brahma (inner), the inner ring (12 Devtas) and the outer band (32 Devtas). The outer 32 are 11.25° wedges numbered clockwise from NE: E1–E8 = 14–21, S1–S8 = 22–29, W1–W8 = 30–37, N1–N8 = 38–45 (E1 starts at 45° from North). The inner ring uses the same compass sectors: each side Devta spans ±26.565° around its cardinal direction, and each corner pair splits at the diagonal. Names follow your reference sheet; edit NAMES in the script to change spelling.
